New `ON_RAMP_ADAPTER` and `ON_RAMP_WEBHOOK_VERIFIER` seam for buying crypto with fiat on a vendor's hosted checkout, delivered to the player's own deposit address. `GET /wallet/on-ramp/options` and `POST /wallet/on-ramp/quote` return the vendor's fiat limits and estimates. `POST /wallet/on-ramp/checkout` requires `acknowledged: true` and gates on RG restriction, country access, the vendor's fiat range, deposit-enabled assets, the network's deposit minimum and the player's deposit limit (fiat amount plus open orders) before recording a `wallet_on_ramp_order` row and opening the checkout. `GET /wallet/on-ramp/orders/{purchaseId}` reads the player's own order, refreshed from the vendor while open. `POST /wallet/on-ramp/webhook` moves the order's status and audits it, and never credits the ledger - the on-chain deposit does. Every route has its own per-player throttle. An adapter throws `OnRampVendorUnavailableError` on an outage; unbound or unavailable, every route refuses. `RgLimitDecision` and `RgLimitExceededData` gain a `currency` field.
