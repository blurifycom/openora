New `ON_RAMP_ADAPTER` and `ON_RAMP_WEBHOOK_VERIFIER` seam for buying crypto with fiat on a vendor's hosted checkout, delivered to the player's own deposit address. `GET /wallet/on-ramp/options` and `POST /wallet/on-ramp/quote` return the vendor's fiat limits and estimates. `POST /wallet/on-ramp/checkout` requires `acknowledged: true` and gates on RG restriction, country access, the vendor's fiat range and offered assets, deposit-enabled assets, the player's deposit limit (fiat amount plus open orders, a pending one until its checkout's `expiresAt`) and the network's deposit minimum before recording a `wallet_on_ramp_order` row and opening the checkout. `GET /wallet/on-ramp/orders/{purchaseId}` reads the player's own order, refreshed from the vendor while open. `POST /wallet/on-ramp/webhook` moves the order's status and audits it, and never credits the ledger - the on-chain deposit does. Options, quote and checkout share one per-player throttle bucket (`on-ramp`), separate from wallet mutations; reading an order has its own (`on-ramp-status`), and the vendor status behind it is cached per order for ten seconds when `CACHE` is bound. An adapter throws `OnRampVendorUnavailableError` on an outage; unbound or unavailable, every route refuses. `RgLimitExceededData` gains a `currency` field, and the refusal branch of `RgLimitDecision` (`allowed: false`) gains a required `currency: string | null`: a custom RG limit gate bound in place of the default must set it on every refusal it returns.
