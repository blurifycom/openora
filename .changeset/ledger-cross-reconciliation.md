The wallet reconciliation cycle now holds the ledger against what other services said it should hold, and reports every gap as a finding. The two ledger kinds stay open until a super admin dismisses them; the other four close themselves once the condition clears, audited as the system. None of the checks moves money, and one that fails is logged and counted while the rest of the run goes on.

- `ledger_request_mismatch`: a Ledger API request whose answered transaction is missing, or differs in type, currency, key, amount or player. `ledger_api_request` now records the `user_id` and `amount` each request moved; rows from before stay null and skip those two comparisons.
- `ledger_unrequested_transaction`: a bet, win or reversal keyed by a provider with no request under its key, counted only from that provider's first request on, so its legacy rows are never flagged.
- `play_eligibility_drift` and `rg_limits_drift`: the ledger projection is behind its owner's version, or differs from it at the same version. Identity and compliance provide the new sealed `PLAY_ELIGIBILITY_SOURCE` and `RG_LIMITS_SOURCE` ports; without them the check is skipped. A projection still ahead of its owner with another state, once the owner is read again, is drift too. Each drifting player is re-published through the owner's own writer, and the finding closes the next cycle that finds them in step; a drift that returns after its finding closed or was dismissed is filed again. Both source ports gain `snapshots(userIds)` and `repair(userIds)`.
- `outbox_relay_lag` and `event_dead_letters`: the oldest unrelayed outbox event is older than `wallet.reconciliation.outboxLagAlertMinutes` (default 5), or the broker's dead-letter streams hold more than `wallet.reconciliation.deadLetterAlertThreshold` entries (default 0). One finding per incident, never one per event, closed once the backlog clears. `MessageBrokerAdapter` gains an optional `deadLetters()`, returning the count and the newest dead letter, implemented by the Redis Streams broker.

Each check that files anything raises one `wallet.reconciliation.alert` per cycle through the outbox; an event pipeline incident also raises one straight on the broker, best effort. The six kinds are new values of `WALLET_RECONCILIATION_FINDING_KINDS`; the wallet migration adds them to the finding kind enum, two nullable columns and an index on `ledger_api_request.created_at`.
